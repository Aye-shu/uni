ABOUT UNIBUS

A SMARTER WAY
TO CONNECT
STUDENTS WITH
TRANSPORTATION.

UniBus is designed around a simple problem: open-credit students do not all follow the same class schedule, but traditional university transportation often does.

Our goal is to bring bus search, seat reservation, return booking, and live tracking into one platform.
